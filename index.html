Method: a synthetic ECG (Gaussian P-QRS-T model) is streamed and corrupted by injected faults. Every 200 ms seven features are computed on the latest 2 s window (NaN ratio, standard deviation, clipping ratio, range, 50 Hz Goertzel power ratio, block-mean drift, median absolute first difference). A priority rule classifier names the fault, and SQI blends the worst and average feature penalties.
